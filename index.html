Quick guide

How to use this practice pack

Practice one task at a time, keep your progress in this browser, and copy your answers for feedback when you want a deeper explanation.

1

Choose your focus

Pick Reading, Listening, Writing, or Speaking, then choose the part you want to practice from the left navigation.

2

Answer and check

Multiple-choice tasks can be checked in the app. Open writing and speaking tasks are saved for review but are not auto-graded.

3

Use the study tools

The timer changes automatically by module. Completed tasks are marked, and you can reset the current task, module, or everything.

4

Get AI feedback

Edit the AI instruction if you want, then copy your task and answers into ChatGPT or another AI app for feedback and explanation.
